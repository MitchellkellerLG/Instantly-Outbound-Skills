Build a one-page website: a walkthrough page for Fernhill Socks, prepared by Example Creative Co.
A prospect from Fernhill Socks lands on it after asking for 5 ad creatives. Its one job is to get them to book a walkthrough call (15 minutes).
Every button on the page links to https://agency.example/book/walkthrough.

Rules:
- Use only the copy below, word for word. Do not invent results, reviews, numbers, testimonials, client logos or customer names, and do not add sections beyond the ones listed.
- Each proof point shows its source under it, as written.
- In the page settings, hide the page from search engines (noindex, nofollow).
- The footer always reads: "A walkthrough page prepared for Fernhill Socks by Example Creative Co. Fernhill Socks names and marks belong to their owner."
- The page is in the brand's look but is not their website: no links to their other pages, no copy taken from their site beyond what is below.

- Light layout, rounded cards, generous spacing.
